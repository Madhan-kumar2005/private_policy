Privacy Policy for FocusFort

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use FocusFort ("we", "us", "our"), developed by Madhan Kumar S. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the privacy policies and practices of the FocusFort Android application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

FocusFort is designed to help you maintain productivity by blocking distracting applications. To provide this core functionality, FocusFort requires specific, sensitive device permissions:

Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

What we collect: We request Usage Access permission to monitor which applications are currently running in the foreground on your device.

Why we need it: This information is strictly used in real-time to determine if the currently active application is on your personalized "blocked" list during an active Focus Session.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

What we collect: This permission allows us to draw interfaces on top of other running applications.

Why we need it: When our system detects that you have opened a distracting app during a focus session, we use this permission to display the FocusFort blocking screen over the distracting app, preventing access.

2. Data Storage, Security, and Sharing

We believe your focus data is your business. FocusFort operates entirely offline for its core blocking features and does not transmit your personal data, app usage statistics, or blocking history to our servers.

  • Local Storage: All app usage monitoring happens locally on your device. Your profiles, PIN codes, settings, and session statistics are saved entirely within your device's local database.
  • No Third-Party Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services (Azure OpenAI)

FocusFort utilizes the Azure OpenAI API specifically for generating AI quizzes in "Student Mode" and evaluating emergency passes in "Office Mode."

  • Only the text prompts you explicitly type during these specific interactions are sent to Azure OpenAI.
  • No personal identifiable information or usage data is sent to OpenAI.
  • Please refer to Microsoft's Privacy Statement for more information on how Azure processes data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Because FocusFort stores all your usage data and settings locally on your physical device rather than on our servers, you have complete control over your data.

How to delete your data: You can permanently delete all data collected and stored by FocusFort at any time by either:

  1. Going to your Android device Settings > Apps > FocusFort > Storage > "Clear Data".
  2. Uninstalling the FocusFort application from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
